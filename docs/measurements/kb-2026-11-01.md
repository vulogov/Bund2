# The occurs check's cost by list length, and a head singleton's skip

Measured 2026-11-01 on macOS (Darwin 27.0.0), arm64, an Apple M5 Max, with
rustc 1.95.0, in a release build (`cargo bench`). Bund2 is commit 375db10,
first as it stands and then with D237's skip. It follows
`kb-2026-10-30.md`, which found the occurs check to be about 31% of naive
reverse of 30 elements, and guessed why without measuring it.

## The question

Naive reverse binds a fresh clause variable to the rest of a list: `T` in
`[ nrev [ H | T ] R ]`, `L` in `[ app [ H | T ] L [ H | R ] ]`. If the
occurs check walks that rest on every such binding, its cost grows with the
list, and its share of a run should grow with the length.

## How it was run

The `kb` bench gains `nrev15x…`, `nrev60x…` and `nrev120x…` beside
`nrev30x10000`: the same query at each length, repeated to about 5,000,000
inferences, so the runs are alike in size and differ in length only.

Three rounds, each a paired A/B on an otherwise idle machine:

    cargo bench -p bund2-bench --bench kb -- nrev --save-baseline <name>
    cargo bench -p bund2-bench --bench kb --features kb-unchecked -- nrev --baseline <name>

`kb-unchecked` compiles the occurs check out (D236), so the change is the
check's whole cost. Each group takes 10 samples; a time is criterion's mean.

| Group | Inferences a run |
|---|---|
| `nrev15x36764` | 5,073,433 |
| `nrev30x10000` | 4,980,001 |
| `nrev60x2644` | 5,005,093 |
| `nrev120x677` | 4,998,292 |

## Before: the check as it stood (375db10)

| Length | With the check (rounds 1–3) | Without it | Change (rounds 1–3) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 15 | 1.964, 1.944, 1.966 s | 1.446, 1.470, 1.513 s | −26.4%, −24.4%, −23.0% |
| 30 | 2.158, 2.127, 2.168 s | 1.459, 1.509, 1.484 s | −32.4%, −29.1%, −31.6% |
| 60 | 2.549, 2.493, 2.570 s | 1.453, 1.481, 1.516 s | −43.0%, −40.6%, −41.0% |
| 120 | 3.180, 3.090, 3.240 s | 1.453, 1.472, 1.526 s | −54.3%, −52.4%, −52.9% |

**The guess holds.** Without the check a run takes about 1.45–1.5 s at every
length; with it, the time grows with the length, and the check's share goes
from about a quarter at 15 to over half at 120. The check walks the tail it
binds, so naive reverse, quadratic in inferences already, paid a second
quadratic term in the check.

## After: D237's skip

A clause variable that occurs once in its head is fresh from the renaming
and occurs nowhere else, so binding it during head unification cannot close
a cycle; `unify_head` skips the walk for those variables only. Naive
reverse's tails are all such variables.

| Length | With the check (rounds 1–3) | Without it | Change (rounds 1–3) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 15 | 2.058, 1.859, 1.719 s | 1.628, 1.700, 1.544 s | −20.9%, −8.5%, −10.2% |
| 30 | 1.806, 1.828, 1.719 s | 1.658, 1.712, 1.542 s | −8.2%, −6.4%, −10.3% |
| 60 | 1.809, 2.012, 1.726 s | 1.663, 1.717, 1.545 s | −8.1%, −14.7%, −10.5% |
| 120 | 1.963, 1.871, 1.738 s | 1.624, 1.625, 1.547 s | −17.3%, −13.1%, −11.0% |

Rounds 1 and 2 were noisy: their intervals are several times round 3's
(`nrev15x…` round 1, [1.949, 2.172] s). Round 3's are within ±1%, and it is
the one to read:

- **The time no longer grows with the length**: 1.72–1.74 s at every
  length, against 1.97–3.24 s before.
- **What the check still costs is flat**, about 10–11% at every length: the
  bindings it still checks are of small terms (a caller's variable bound to
  `[ H | R ]`), and the rest is the skip's own bookkeeping, the renaming's
  map looked up for each singleton on each clause tried.
- **Against before**, round 3: 30 elements from 2.168 to 1.719 s (−21%),
  120 from 3.240 to 1.738 s (−46%).

The build without the check is slower after than before (round 3: 1.54
against 1.45–1.53 s), because the skip's bookkeeping runs whether or not the
check is compiled in. That is a cost to the measurement build only.

## What it does not show

- **Other programs.** A head variable bound to a long term, and occurring
  twice in its head, is still walked each time; so is any binding made by
  `=` or in a body. Naive reverse is the program criterion 16 names, not a
  survey.
- **That the skip is sound.** That is an argument, D237's, and a test,
  `a_head_singleton_needs_no_occurs_check`, which finds the full check and
  the skip agree over 625 pairings of heads and calls.
