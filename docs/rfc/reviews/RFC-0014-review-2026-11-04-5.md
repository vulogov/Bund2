first, and the earlier reviews only afterwards, to drop findings already

Revision 20 was checked hardest (`git diff 7054f77 52db05a` on the RFC

- §S6's bullet, now "A ranked query answers `"scope"`", with the
  traversal-or-closure sentence and "A packed query keeps its kind";
- §S7's "The scope": `parse`, `plan` and `run` taken directly, `run`'s

- §S7's `effective` paragraph, with the lapse on an appended ground;
- the Preservation row "A fact a proof uses";
- criterion 9's two fixtures and criterion 11's two new cases;
- D244's revision 20 note on R8.

`run` was followed into `run_pipeline`, `order`, `shape` and
`supersede`; the parser's stage loop and `lower_shaping` were read to
see which stage orders a query can have; `supersede` into
`supersession_heads`; the fact order into `Kb::build`.

**Verdict: Ready once S1 and S2 are answered. 0 blocking, 2 should-fix,
4 notes.** Revision 20's answers to the eighteenth review hold against
the source: a `pack` stage does run after the supersession pass, the
lapse of an `effective` fact is stated correctly and carried into the
Preservation row and D244, and `smysl.kb` now reports what `run` warns.
Two gaps remain. §S6's account of when an answer keeps its kind is still
an incomplete list: an ordering stage, a set operation, or a `limit`
after `pack` all give `"scope"` too (S1). And the rule revision 20 wrote
for unheld uids covers `smysl.kb` only; the `smysl` conditional, which
pushes "the unit's MAP" for each uid of the answer, has no rule for a uid
with no unit (S2).

## Should fix

### S1. An answer loses its kind at more stages than §S6 lists: an ordering stage, a set operation, and a `limit` after `pack`

**Location.** §S6, the bullet "A ranked query answers `"scope"`" (lines
1398-1408), in particular "It does so too when the query has a traversal
or a closure" and "A packed query keeps its kind"; criterion 9 ("the
ranked query has no traversal or closure, so it keeps its kind", line
2263); §S7's "A `ranked` answer … in a library holding a supersession is
already a `scope` answer" (lines 1463-1465). Revision 20 text, refining
revision 19's.

**Evidence.**

- **Ordering.** Every `order` arm returns `Answer::Scope` or
  `Answer::Groups`, whatever it was given: `Salience` (`smysl-query/src/exec.rs:1035`),
  `Field` (`:1046`), `Keys` (`:1096`), `GroupBy`/`CompareBy` (`:1106`).
  `run` applies the ordering stages to every answer in phase 9
  (`:124-126`). So `~lex "x" order by salience descending` answers
  `"scope"` in any library, with no scores.
- **Set operations.** `run_expr` answers `Answer::Scope(set_op(…))` for
  `union`, `intersect` and `except` (`:169-173`), in uid order
  (`:185-192`), whatever the operands were.
- **`limit` after `pack`.** `shape`'s `Limit` arm keeps `Ranked` and
  `Groups` and turns anything else, `Packed` included, into
  `Answer::Scope` (`:1153-1177`, the `other` arm at `:1172-1176`). The
  parser accepts `limit` and `pack` in either order, refusing only a
  shaping stage after the output stage (`smysl-query/src/parse.rs:287-295`),
  and `lower_shaping` refuses only `pack … as view`
  (`smysl-query/src/plan.rs:1360-1387`). So `… pack 500 tokens limit 5`
  answers `"scope"`. ("A packed query keeps its kind" is right about the
  supersession pass, which is what the eighteenth review's S1 asked.)
- Filters and time stages keep `Ranked` (`:252-257`, `:283-297`), and
  `limit` keeps it (`:1159-1162`).

**Consequence.** The bullet is the third revision in a row to extend
the list one condition at a time, and criterion 9's "so it keeps its
kind" still reads the list as complete. An implementer writing
criterion 9's ranked query with an `order by`, or its packed query with
a `limit`, gets `"scope"` against a test that expects otherwise; a
program author reading §S6 expects scores after `order by`.

**Needed.** State the rule positively rather than by exceptions, for
example: "A `ranked` answer keeps its kind only through filters, time
stages and `limit`, and only in a library holding no supersession; a
traversal, a closure, a set operation or an ordering stage gives a
`scope` (or, for `group by`/`compare by`, a `groups`) answer
(`smysl-query/src/exec.rs:169-173,311-315,426,1007-1109`). A `packed`
answer is built after the pass (`:1179-1213`) and keeps its kind unless
a `limit` follows it (`:1172-1176`)." Criterion 9: the ranked query has
no traversal, closure, set operation or ordering stage, and the packed
query no `limit` after `pack`.

### S2. The `smysl` conditional has no rule for a uid with no unit, which revision 20 shows an answer routinely names

**Location.** §S6, the `smysl` row ("It runs the lambda once per unit of
the answer, with the unit's MAP on top", line 1380) and "The conditional
visits the units `Answer::uids` names" (line 1392); criterion 9. Older
text; revision 20 makes the case ordinary by its own citation.

**Evidence.**

- Revision 20 says, for `smysl.kb`, that "A `grounds` closure adds every
  ground of a seed, held or not" (`smysl-query/src/exec.rs:414-418`), and
  leaves such a uid out of the scope. Revision 19 made an unheld ground
  ordinary (§S2 lets `smysl.unit` name one).
- Other sources of an unheld uid in an answer, one level further down:
  `supersede` maps a unit to its head (`:487-488`), and the head is a
  `supersedes` relation's `from` (`smysl-graph/src/relink.rs:167-168`),
  which `smysl.rel` does not require to be held (§S4); a forked unit maps
  to its `direct_successors` (`exec.rs:489-491`), likewise. `trace_via`
  pushes every uid `supports_of` returns without a presence check
  (`smysl-graph/src/lineage.rs:257-266`).
- §S6's conditional visits `Answer::uids` and pushes "the unit's MAP".
  For such a uid there is no unit, so no §S2 MAP. Nothing says whether
  the runner skips it, pushes NODATA (as `smysl.get` answers for an
  absent uid, line 1320), pushes the bare uid, or refuses. `smysl.query`'s
  `units` list will name the uid, which is fine for a list of uids but
  is also worth one sentence, since `smysl.kb` over the same text now
  names a different set.

**Needed.** Decide the conditional's rule and state it beside the
`count` bullet: most consistent with revision 20 is "a uid the answer
names that is not a unit the store holds is not visited, as `smysl.kb`
leaves it out", or else NODATA on top. Say that `smysl.query`'s `units`
and `hits` keep such uids. Add a criterion 9 case: a `grounds` closure
from a unit with an unheld ground, under the conditional, runs the
lambda the stated number of times.

## Notes

- **N1. §S6 names only `parse` and `plan` as refusing a query; `run`
  refuses too.** (§S6, "A query that `parse` or `plan` refuses is SM04",
  line 1412; criterion 9's SM04 case.) `run` returns `Err` with
  `SMY-E460` for a `pack` budget the packer cannot meet
  (`smysl-query/src/exec.rs:1192-1197`), an unbound `$depth`
  (`:342-346`), an unbound `$name` entity (`:965-973`) and an unbound
  `$count` (`:1219-1230`); `PlanOptions`' own comment puts an unbound
  parameter's refusal "at execution and not at planning"
  (`smysl-query/src/plan.rs:121-122`). §S7 now says "a refusal from any
  step is SM04", and §S8's SM04 row ("`sq/1` refused the query") covers
  it, so only §S6's wording lags. Suggest "`parse`, `plan` or `run`".
  Likewise the revision 20 answer table's "`SMY-E460` … no longer
  arises" (line 3112) is true only of the saved-query unit's `SMY-E460`.
- **N2. The fact order sentence fixes the wrong thing.** (§S7, "the KB
  holds their facts in that order, the `rank` facts last", lines
  1449-1450.) `Kb::build` files each clause under its predicate key in a
  `BTreeMap` and keeps order only within a predicate
  (`crates/bund2-stdlib/src/kb/store.rs:223-237`). So "the `rank` facts
  last" has no effect, and what matters is the order within each
  predicate. For `unit`, `gist`, `effective` and the rest the answer's
  order defines it; for `rel` facts it does not, since a
  relation has two ends in scope. No criterion checks any order.
  Suggest naming `rel`'s order (for example `Store::relations` order, or
  by `From`'s position then `To`'s) and dropping "the `rank` facts last",
  or adding an order check to criterion 11.
- **N3. "by Bund2 or a bridge while the proof runs" narrows who can
  append the ground.** (§S7, line 1551.) Any writer can: another Bund2
  process, a SMYSL command that locks, or one of the six that do not
  (R22). The withdrawn-edge sentence just above names "another process".
  Suggest "by any writer, a bridge while the proof runs included".
- **N4. "M8's first step" names a step M8 does not have.** (§S7, line
  1458; the revision 19 answer table, line 3104.) M8's row (line 2061)
  builds `smysl.kb` and claims criterion 11; it lists no investigation
  of what a `from texts` answer names, unlike M1's "First, Q55's spike".
  Revision 20's left-out rule makes the answer harmless to `smysl.kb`
  either way, so suggest dropping the sentence, or adding the step to
  M8's row.

## Assumptions the RFC makes but does not state

1. **Only a traversal, a closure or a supersession takes a ranked
   answer's scores** (S1). Ordering stages and set operations do too.
2. **A packed answer is final** (S1). A following `limit` makes it a
   scope.
3. **Every uid the conditional visits has a unit** (S2). A `grounds`
   closure, a supersession head, or a `rests-on` traversal can name one
   that has none.
4. **The KB's clause order is the order facts are handed to it** (N2).
   It is per predicate; across predicates there is no order.
5. **An answer's uids are distinct.** True at 4f5ecb9 for every variant
   (see "Previous answers checked", S3), and what "one per unit in
   scope" relies on now that `resolve`'s `BTreeSet` is gone. Worth one
   clause, since it is SMYSL's property and not Bund2's.
