# RFC-0014 review: Draft revision 15, 2026-11-03 (fourteenth review)

Adversarial review, independent of the thirteen before it. The RFC and
the sources were read first, and the earlier reviews only afterwards, to
drop findings already raised and answered. Nothing was edited or built,
apart from running `cargo xtask cite`. Read against Bund2's working tree
at 4087d3e, the `reference/` submodules (whose `git status` is empty),
and SMYSL at 4f5ecb9 ("Merge 1.14.0"), opened file by file. SMYSL
citations are relative to its crate directory, as the RFC writes them.

Revisions 14 and 15 were checked hardest
(`git diff d33e7ef c6afa82` and `git diff c6afa82 4087d3e` on the RFC
and `decisions.md`):

- `smysl.write`'s rewritten "What it passes": `Store::iter` less
  attestations, relations one per rid;
- `smysl.parse`'s new rule that a relation the document withdraws is
  left out;
- `smysl.kb`'s `rel` facts and `is_withdrawn`;
- §S4's withdrawn-edge `Warning` on re-writing a relation;
- the handle's absolute root (SM00) and D244's dated note on R3;
- §Current's new "Which variant the store keeps" and "A withdrawn
  relation" bullets.

`smysl.write` was followed into `write_surface`, `has_surface_form`,
`withdrawal_has_surface_form`, `manifest_has_surface_form`,
`write_relation` and `write_unit`. The withdrawal rules were followed
into `Withdrawal` (`lifecycle.rs`), `Store::is_withdrawn`, the parser's
`lifecycle_record` and its lifecycle pass, the check pass that raises
`SMY-W056`, and SMYSL's `cmd_withdraw` and `persist`. The variant rule
was followed into `Store::from_records`, `absorb_hashed` and

**Verdict: Ready once S1 is answered. 0 blocking, 1 should-fix,
10 notes.** D244 and D245 are not re-argued. Revision 15's answers to
the thirteenth review hold against the source, with one gap. "A relation
the document withdraws" and "the edge is withdrawn" are applied to every
kind. SMYSL exempts `supersedes` and `retracts`: a withdrawal of either
"is kept and ignored", and "the edge simply goes on applying" (S1).

## Should fix

### S1. A withdrawal of a `supersedes` or `retracts` edge has no effect in SMYSL, but `smysl.parse` and §S4's `Warning` treat it as one

Revision 15 adds two rules:

- **`smysl.parse`:** "**A relation the document withdraws is left out
  too**, with a `Warning` counting such relations … answering the
  relation would let `smysl.append` write live an edge the document
  says nothing should follow (`smysl-core/src/types/lifecycle.rs:18-22`)."
- **§S4, re-writing a relation:** "If the edge is withdrawn, it stays
  withdrawn … and the word reports a `Warning` that the edge it answers
  is withdrawn."

Neither rule names an exception. The Preservation row "A withdrawn
edge" says "Kept withdrawn", also without one.

**Evidence.**

- The comment the RFC cites ends with the exception: "`retracts` and
  `supersedes` edges cannot be withdrawn in 1.4 (`SMY-W056`)"
  (`smysl-core/src/types/lifecycle.rs:22`).
- `Store::is_withdrawn` applies it:
  `!rel.kind.is_lifecycle() && self.interp.withdrawals.contains_key(&rel.uid())`
  (`smysl-graph/src/store/mod.rs:1500-1504`). `is_lifecycle` is
  `Supersedes | Retracts` (`smysl-core/src/types/relation.rs:158-160`).
- SMYSL's check says what such a withdrawal means. "The withdrawal is
  kept and ignored, which a reader would otherwise have no way to learn:
  the edge simply goes on applying" (`SMY-W056`,
  `smysl-check/src/passes/integrity.rs:40-59`; severity `Warn`,
  `smysl-core/src/diag.rs:271`).
- The parser does not refuse one. `lifecycle_record` accepts any kind
  `RelKind::parse` accepts (`smysl-core/src/surface/parse.rs:661-687`),
  and the lifecycle pass turns it into a `Withdrawal` of that rid
  (`:2651-2685`). Neither raises `SMY-W056`; only `check` does. SMYSL's
  `withdraw` command refuses such an edge (`smysl:src/main.rs:4916-4923`),
  but a document or an import can still carry the record.

**Consequences, as written.**

- A document holding `@rel a --supersedes--> b` and
  `@withdraw a --supersedes--> b { … }` is answered by `smysl.parse`
  without the `supersedes` relation. `smysl.append` then writes a
  library without an edge that SMYSL would follow. That is the
  opposite of what the rule was added for.
- A document holding a `retracts` relation that it also "withdraws"
  loses the relation in `smysl.parse`. `smysl.append` would have refused
  that document with SM03 [R23, criterion 5's sixth document]. It now
  appends the rest silently, and the library lacks a retraction SMYSL
  would apply.
- On `smysl.rel` of a held `supersedes` edge whose rid a withdrawal
  names, an implementer who tests "a withdrawal names the rid" reports a
  `Warning` that the edge is withdrawn. SMYSL reads the edge as live.
  The `Warning` is wrong.
- `smysl.kb` is not affected. It names `is_withdrawn`, which applies
  the exception.

**Needed.**

- State both rules in terms of `is_withdrawn`'s reading. A relation is
  withdrawn when it is not `supersedes` or `retracts` and a withdrawal
  names its rid. For `smysl.parse`, which has no store, say that the
  same test is applied to the document's records.
- Say what `smysl.parse` does with a withdrawal of a lifecycle edge.
  It keeps the relation and skips the withdrawal, as for any other
  record. Consider also a `Warning` carrying `SMY-W056`'s meaning,
  since nothing else will raise it.
- Correct the Preservation row "A withdrawn edge" to the same rule.
- Add a case to criterion 18 in M4: a document that withdraws a
  `supersedes` edge. `smysl.parse` answers the relation, and the
  `Warning` for left-out relations does not count it.

- **N3. Where a library's withdrawals come from.** SMYSL's `withdraw`
  writes to a store file, as `retract` does (Citations, above). At
  4f5ecb9 a withdrawal reaches a library through the API, or through a
  command that opens the library itself, such as those under
  `with_library` (`smysl:src/main.rs:8600-8628`). This review did not
  establish which of those carry withdrawals. Criteria 11 and 18
  already build theirs through the API, so nothing is untestable. But
  §Current's sentence implies that the CLI withdraws in a library, and
  §S4 says a program "has no way" to change a weight "as SMYSL has
  none", citing `withdraw`. Say that `withdraw` acts on a store file.
  Then the reader learns that neither Bund2 nor SMYSL's CLI can withdraw
  an edge in a library directly.
- **N4. A withdrawal with no surface form leaves its edge written
  live.**
  - `withdrawal_has_surface_form` requires an empty `extra` and
    `ts.agent == agent` (`smysl-core/src/surface/write.rs:236-240`).
  - A withdrawal that fails it is counted in the per-kind `Warning`.
    Its relation is still written, since `write_surface` writes every
    relation (`:161`).
  - So the text reads that edge as live. Neither `smysl.write`'s row
    ("a withdrawn edge is written with its withdrawal and reads back
    withdrawn") nor the Preservation row ("Kept withdrawn") says so.
  - Qualify both rows. Name the case in the `Warning`: a withdrawal left
    out whose edge is written. Criterion 18's withdrawn-edge fixture
    should state that its withdrawal is built with `ts.agent` equal to
    `agent`, or the test depends on how the fixture's clock was built.
- **N5. "Reads back withdrawn" and "Kept withdrawn" describe SMYSL's
  reader, not Bund2's.**
  - Through `smysl.parse`, revision 15's own rule leaves the edge out.
  - So `smysl.write` → `smysl.parse` → `smysl.append` gives a library
    with no such edge, not one where it is withdrawn.
  - The Preservation row should say "absent from a copy" for
    `smysl.parse` and `smysl.append`. "Kept withdrawn" should be kept
    for SMYSL's own `parse_surface`.
- **N6. A relation's second variant is dropped by `smysl.write` without
  a `Warning`.** `smysl.write` passes `Store::relations` in place of
  every relation record. A second record under one rid, with another
  weight or note, is therefore never judged by `has_surface_form`. It
  is in no count. The meaning is unchanged, since no interpreting read
  sees it (§Current). But the row promises that what is left out is
  said, and SMYSL's `merge` would have written it. Count it in the
  `Warning` for dropped `extra`, or state the exception.
- **N7. Where the relations go in the record sequence.** `write_surface`
  writes units, relations and threads in the order the records are
  given (`write.rs:158-165`). §S4 replaces the relation records of
  `Store::iter` with `Store::relations`. It does not say where they go:
  in the place of each rid's first record, or after the other records.
  The parser re-orders on reading (`parse.rs:2585-2615`), so the
  meaning is the same either way. The text is not. Criterion 17's
  `expected.txt` pins any text the demo prints. Name the placement.
- **N8. A relation whose rid is already withdrawn before it arrives.**
  SMYSL keeps "withdrawals by the rid they name, whether or not that
  relation has arrived" (`smysl-graph/src/store/interp.rs:31-32`). So
  `smysl.rel` of a new edge whose rid a withdrawal already names
  appends it, and it is withdrawn on arrival. §S4's `Warning` is stated
  only for "a relation whose rid the library holds". Say whether the
  `Warning` applies to every relation the word answers that
  `is_withdrawn` reports, which is the simpler rule and covers both
  cases.
- **N9. The handle's absolute root: one bullet and one case.**
  - §S2's "Every word validates the handle it is given. That covers
    `type`, a STRING `root`, and an `agent` …" omits the absolute root
    that the bullet above it and SM00's row require. Add it.
  - Criterion 14 lists no relative-root case for SM00. Add one in M2:
    `smysl.agent`, and a word that opens, given a hand-built handle with
    a relative root, are SM00 and do no I/O. Criterion 15's "two
    spellings of its root" must then both be absolute. Say so, since
    the obvious second spelling is a relative one.
- **N10. `smysl.prove`'s stand-for rule and revision 15's `rel` facts.**
  - §S7 says a fact "whose uid arguments all name units in the
    handle's library, stands for those units", judged "at write time".
  - Revision 15 makes `smysl.kb` load no `rel` fact for a withdrawn
    edge. But a `[ rel "rebuts" X C ]` fact still stands for `X` and

    KB loaded before a withdrawal, or built by `kb!`, supplies one.
  - Rule M still holds, since grounds are units and their declared
    statuses are read from the library. But the conclusion is written
    as resting on an edge SMYSL no longer follows.
  - Either:
    - require, for the `rel` shape, that the library hold that relation
      and that `is_withdrawn` be false, and otherwise treat the fact as
      a program fact, which matches `smysl.kb`; or
    - state that the stand-for rule checks only that the uids are

  Also, criterion 11 counts `rel` facts "against `Store::relations`
  less what `is_withdrawn` reports". It omits §S7's "both ends in
  scope". Name the scope, or add the filter, so the count is
  well-defined.

## Assumptions the RFC makes but does not state

1. **Every withdrawal withdraws its edge** (S1). SMYSL ignores a
   withdrawal of a `supersedes` or `retracts` edge.
2. **Every withdrawal a library holds has a surface form** (N4).
   Otherwise `smysl.write` writes its edge live.
3. **A library's withdrawals come from SMYSL's `withdraw` command**
   (N3). That command writes a store file. A library gains withdrawals
   through the API, or through a command that opens the library itself.
4. **Dropping a relation's second variant needs no `Warning`** (N6).
5. **Where `Store::relations` goes in the records given to
   `write_surface` does not matter** (N7). It does not change meaning,
   but it changes the text.
6. **Only a relation the library already holds can arrive withdrawn**
   (N8). A withdrawal may name a rid before its relation arrives.
7. **A `rel` fact whose ends are library units stands for a live edge**
   (N10). §S7 checks only that the uids are present.
