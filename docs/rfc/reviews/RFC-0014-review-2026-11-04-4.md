# RFC-0014 review: Draft revision 19, 2026-11-04 (eighteenth review)

Adversarial review of revision 19. The RFC and the sources were read
first, and the earlier reviews only afterwards, to drop findings already
raised and answered. Nothing was edited or built, apart from running
`cargo xtask cite`. Read against Bund2's working tree at 7054f77, and
SMYSL at 4f5ecb9, opened file by file. SMYSL citations are relative to
its crate directory, as the RFC writes them.

Revision 19 was checked hardest (`git diff a6d4a7a 7054f77` on the RFC
and `docs/registers/decisions.md`):

- §S6's new bullet, "A ranked or packed query answers `"scope"`";
- §S7's scope bullet, now naming `scope::resolve`, its SM04 refusal and
  "a uid the answer names that is not a unit the store holds gives no
  fact";
- §S7's table rows ("per unit in scope", "held or not");
- §S7's stand-for test, with `G` and `D` no longer required to be held,
  and the "at write time" paragraph after it;
- the Preservation row "A fact a proof uses";
- criterion 9's second fixture and criterion 18's unheld-ground case;
- D244's second dated note on R8.

`scope::resolve` was followed into `run`, `run_pipeline`, `shape` and
`supersede`; the `effective` claim into `effective_status`'s orphaning
loop; the unheld-uid rule into the traversal and closure phase.

**Verdict: Ready once S1–S3 are answered. 0 blocking, 3 should-fix,
4 notes.** The three design choices revision 19 put to the owner are
sound in what they decide: requiring only `U`, `From` and `To` to be held
matches how SMYSL keeps and reads a unit with an absent ground;
`scope::resolve` is the call `measure` makes; and a ranked query does
become a scope in a library holding a supersession. But the §S6 bullet
extends that last point to packed queries, which it does not reach (S1);
the "stays borne out" claim for `effective` facts is false once an absent
ground is appended (S2); and `scope::resolve` discards the run's warnings,
which §S8 says are reported (S3).

## Should fix

### S1. A packed query never answers `"scope"`: `pack` runs after the supersession pass

**Location.** §S6, the bullet "A ranked or packed query answers
`"scope"`" (line 1390); the revision 19 answer table, row N4 (line
3075). The Summary's revision 19 line (line 180) says only "a ranked
query", so the RFC also disagrees with itself.

**Evidence.**

- The supersession pass is in `run_pipeline`, phases 5 to 7
  (`smysl-query/src/exec.rs:429-454`).
- `Answer::Packed` is built in one place only, the `ShapePlan::Pack` arm
  of `shape` (`:1179-1213`). `shape` runs in phase 9, in `run`, after
  `run_expr` has returned (`:122-128`).
- So no `Packed` answer ever reaches `supersede`. Its `Packed` arm
  (`:501`) is unreachable in practice; SMYSL's comment on the next arm
  says as much of `Count` and `Groups` ("produced in phase 9, after this
  pass", `:509-511`), and the same holds of `Packed`.
- A `pack N tokens` query therefore answers `Packed` in any library,
  with or without a supersession. It packs the units the pass left
  (`:1180-1185` takes `answer.uids()`).

**Consequence.** An implementer writing criterion 9's second fixture from
§S6 would add a packed query expecting `"scope"` and get `"packed"`.
Criterion 9 as written is right (it checks only the ranked query in the
second fixture), so the error is in §S6's prose and the answer table.

**Needed.** Drop "or packed" and "or `Packed`" from §S6's bullet, and say
instead that a `pack` stage runs after the pass, so a packed query keeps
its kind (`:122-128`, `:1179-1213`). A next revision's answer table can
record the correction. Optionally add a packed query to criterion 9's
second fixture, answering `"packed"`.

### S2. A loaded `effective` fact does not stay borne out once an absent ground is appended

**Location.** §S7, the paragraph after the per-shape list ("a retraction
appended since only ever makes a unit `unfounded` … so an `effective`
fact `smysl.kb` loaded stays borne out"); the Preservation row "A fact a
proof uses" ("a fact `smysl.kb` loaded stands for its units at write
time, unless its edge was withdrawn since"); D244's revision 19 note,
same sentence (`docs/registers/decisions.md:16681-16682`).

**Evidence.** The strict policy's orphaning loop
(`smysl-graph/src/merge/retraction.rs:97-124`) makes a unit `unfounded`
when every *present* ground is `unfounded`, and the present grounds are
filtered by `store.contains_uid` (`:103-108`). Revision 19 now lets a
unit's ground be absent. So:

- `U` grounds on `A` and `G`; `A` is retracted; `G` is absent. `U`'s
  present grounds are `[A]`, all `unfounded`, so `U` is orphaned and
  `smysl.kb` loads `[ effective U "unfounded" ]`.
- `G` is then appended, not retracted. `U`'s present grounds are
  `[A, G]`, not all `unfounded`, so `effective_status` gives `U` its
  declared status again.
- At write time, the fact's test (`effective_status` gives `U` that
  status) fails, and the fact is written as a program fact.

The retraction lines cited (`:83-86`, `:112-117`) are right that a
*retraction* only ever moves a unit to `unfounded`; it is an *append of a
unit* that moves one back. Bund2 can do it between `smysl.kb` and
`smysl.prove` (`smysl.unit` or `smysl.append` of `G`, which §S2 accepts),
and so can a bridge while the proof runs, which §S7 says counts. The
same holds transitively: appending a unit anywhere below an orphaned
chain can un-orphan it.

Nothing in the design is wrong: the test is still judged at write time,
and the fact falls to a program fact, which is the safe outcome. The
claim is what is wrong, and it is the claim the Preservation row and the
D244 note repeat.

**Needed.** Qualify the sentence: "an `effective` fact `smysl.kb` loaded
stays borne out under later retractions; a ground absent at load and
appended since can return its unit to its declared status, leaving the
fact a program fact". Carry the same qualifier into the Preservation row
("unless its edge was withdrawn, or a ground it lacked appended, since")
and a further dated note on D244 R8. A criterion 18 case in M9 would pin
it, but is optional.

### S3. `scope::resolve` discards the run's warnings, which §S8 says are reported

**Location.** §S7, "The scope"; §S8, "**Warnings** from `run`, from a
measure's diagnostics, … are each reported as a `Warning` (D36)" (line
1671).

**Evidence.** `resolve` calls `run` (`smysl-measure/src/scope.rs:103`)
and keeps only `run.answer` (`:108`). `Resolved` has no field for
warnings (`:65-74`: `text`, `uids`, `digest`, `basis`). So the warnings a
scope's run raises are dropped before Bund2 sees them. Among them:

- `SMY-W464`, a forked supersession whose successors replaced a unit in
  scope (`smysl-query/src/exec.rs:512-519`), which is exactly the case
  that changes which units `smysl.kb` loads;
- `SMY-W463`, a traversal stopped at its depth (`:399-405`);
- `SMY-W420` and `SMY-W475`, units excluded by a lens or matched without
  a version marker (`:139-148`, `:207-215`).

`smysl.query` reports these, through `run` directly. `smysl.kb`, over the
same text, would report none. `measure` loses them too
(`smysl:src/main.rs:7366`), so "as a measure's is" is faithful to SMYSL,
but it contradicts §S8's sentence for `smysl.kb`, and no criterion says
which reading holds.

**Needed.** One of:

- **(a)** Resolve with `parse`, `plan` and `run` directly, as
  `smysl.query` does, reporting `run`'s warnings; `PlanOptions::default()`
  and §S5's environment keep the scope the same. The saved-query unit is
  then not built, and `SMY-E460` from its build (which `UnitCore::new`
  cannot raise for a parsed query, see Citations) is no longer a case.
- **(b)** Keep `resolve`, and say in §S7 and §S8 that `smysl.kb` reports
  no warning of its scope's run, as SMYSL's `measure` does not.

(a) is the smaller departure from what a program sees from
`smysl.query`. Either way, a criterion 11 case with a forked supersession
in scope would fix the reading (`SMY-W464` reported, or not).

## Notes

- **N1. A ranked query also answers `"scope"` whenever it has a
  traversal or closure, and a converted answer is in uid order.** (§S6's
  bullet; criterion 9.) Phase 7 replaces the answer with
  `Answer::Scope(reached …)` for any traversal or closure
  (`smysl-query/src/exec.rs:311-315,426`), whatever the library holds.
  And `supersede` rebuilds the answer from a `BTreeSet`
  (`:505-506`), so the converted scope is in uid order, not hit order,
  and with a fork may hold more units than the limit (`:491`). Suggest
  §S6 say "a ranked query with no traversal or closure keeps its kind
  only in a library with no supersession", and criterion 9's ranked
  query have neither.
- **N2. A scope can name an unheld uid in an ordinary query, and "gives
  no fact" should say it leaves the scope.** (§S7, "A uid the answer
  names that is not a unit the store holds gives no fact"; criterion 11;
  M8.) The RFC presents this as a question about `from texts`, for M8 to
  settle. It is reachable now: the `grounds` closure adds every ground
  of a seed, held or not (`smysl-query/src/exec.rs:414-418`), and
  revision 19 makes unheld grounds ordinary. Whether "gives no fact"
  also removes the uid from "both ends in scope" for `rel` facts decides
  whether a relation to an absent unit (which `smysl.rel` does not
  refuse, §S4) can be loaded as a `rel` fact that the stand-for test
  then rejects. Suggest "is left out of the scope", and a criterion 11
  case: a grounds-closure scope over a unit with an absent ground loads
  no `unit` fact for the ground.
- **N3. The KB's fact order is uid order, unstated.** `resolve` returns
  the scope as a `BTreeSet` (`smysl-measure/src/scope.rs:68,108`), so the
  answer's order (§S7 cites `Answer::uids`, which has one) is lost.
  A KB keeps its clauses in the order it is built from
  (`crates/bund2-stdlib/src/kb/store.rs:223`), so the order in which
  `smysl.prove` meets them, and plausibly its answers, follows uid order. Worth one line in §S7;
  if S3 (a) is taken, say which order is used.
- **N4. The Preservation row's "ground … the library does not hold" now
  reads two ways.** (Preservation, "A fact a proof uses"; §S7's "no
  conclusion is written as resting on a status, ground, label or edge
  the library does not hold".) After revision 19 a ground need not be
  held, so "a ground the library does not hold" can be read as an
  unheld `G`, which stands. Suggest "a ground or dependency `U` does not
  have".

## Assumptions the RFC makes but does not state

1. **A packed query's kind depends on the library** (S1). It does not:
   `pack` runs after the pass.
2. **No ground absent at `smysl.kb` is appended before the write** (S2).
3. **The scope's run raises no warning worth reporting**, or
   `scope::resolve` passes them on (S3). It drops them.
4. **A ranked query with no supersession keeps its ranking** (N1). Only
   without a traversal or closure.
5. **Only an unusual source names an unheld uid** (N2). The `grounds`
   closure does it routinely.
6. **The KB's facts are in some order a program can rely on** (N3).
   They are in uid order.
