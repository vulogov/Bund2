**Verdict: Not ready. 3 blocking, 7 should-fix, 12 notes.**

Revision 23's answers to B1–B5 hold in what they decide. The new problems are in what the commit path does, one call down:

- **The commit's re-check cannot see a retraction** (B1). Rule M at staging and in `check` both read a ground's *stored* status, never its effective one. Criterion 25's retraction case cannot pass, and the answer to the first review's S5 rests on it.
- **Answers poison the library for later commits** (B2). The answer template writes `derived`; R39 attests it at rung `Model`, whose ceiling is `inferred`. Nothing checks that before the write. Afterwards rule T reports `SMY-E033`, and because `prepare_under` checks the whole library, every later staging and every later `smysl.commit` of that library is refused.
- **Model labels meet SM08** (B3). Every model unit carries a label of the model's choosing. Phase 1's SM08, which R40 applies at commit, refuses the whole batch when the library already binds that label elsewhere. Meanwhile `smysl.ingest.text` writes bindings inside SMYSL with no such check.

## Blocking

### B1. The commit's re-check cannot see a retraction or a supersession, so criterion 25's last case cannot pass and the answer to S5 does not hold

**Location.**
- §P4 step 6 (lines 2440-2445): "a ground retracted or superseded since staging lowers a unit as it would have then, with `SMY-W036`".
- Criterion 25's last bullet (lines 3376-3379): `staged batch no longer checks clean` "after a unit it grounds on is retracted with `smysl.retract`".
- R40 A (line 3634); the answer to S5 (line 4104).

**Evidence.**
- `prepare_under` runs `monotone::apply`, then `check` over an `Overlay` of the store and the batch (`smysl-ingest/src/stage.rs:192,221-230`).
- `monotone::apply` takes each ground's status from `status_of`, which ends `store.get(uid).map(|u| u.core.status)` (`smysl-ingest/src/monotone.rs:61-74`), and lowers only when `core.status > cap` (`:120`).
- `check`'s rule M pass does the same: `weakest_ground` reads `u.core.status` (`smysl-check/src/passes/epistemics.rs:63-68`).
- A unit's core status is part of its identity and is never rewritten. A retraction is a `retracts` edge, read only by `effective_status` (`smysl-graph/src/merge/retraction.rs:70`), which neither pass calls. A superseded unit also keeps its record and status.

So re-running `prepare_under` after `smysl.retract` finds the same statuses it found at staging. It lowers nothing, reports no `W036` and no error, and the commit succeeds. The same is true at staging: a ground retracted *before* staging is not seen either. The first review's S5 premise ("a ground retracted … leaves a unit committed above what its grounds now support") was right about the outcome but assumed rule M would see the retraction. It does not, at either time.

What re-running `prepare_under` does see between staging and commit:
- a ground that was absent and is now held;
- any error the rest of the library has gained (see B2).

**Needed.** A decision on R40, because its A option rests on this:
- (a) Have `smysl.commit` judge grounds by effective status under the locks. Phase 1 already computes effective status for `smysl.kb` and `smysl.prove`. Refuse, or lower, a unit whose ground is now `unfounded` or superseded. This goes beyond SMYSL, which never checks it, so it needs a Preservation row.
- (b) Keep `prepare_under` alone, delete the retraction claim from step 6 and from criterion 25, and add a Preservation row: a ground retracted or superseded before commit is not seen, as SMYSL's `merge --staged` does not see it.

Either way, correct line 4104's answer to S5.

### B2. An answer's units exceed the `Model` rung's ceiling. After its commit, every later staging and commit of that library is refused

**Location.** §P7, R39 A (lines 2614-2628, 3633); §P4 step 6 (lines 2440-2445); criterion 26 (lines 3383-3396).

**Evidence.**
1. **The answer is `derived`.** The answer template tells the model: "Each record is `@claim` with a label of your choosing, `status: derived`" (`smysl-ingest/src/prompt.rs:685-689`), and so does its example (`:701`).
2. **`Model` caps at `inferred`.** `Rung::Model.ceiling()` is `Status::Inferred` (`smysl-core/src/types/provenance.rs:218-224`), and `Inferred` (2) < `Derived` (3) (`smysl-core/src/types/epistemics.rs:21-29`). `Imported` lifts the ceiling only at `Computed` (`provenance.rs:326-331`).
3. **Nothing checks this before the write.**
   - `answer` caps nothing against the rung. It parses, admits and calls `prepare_under` (`answer.rs:245-283`).
   - Document ingest does cap against the rung, in `one_call`, through `repair::check_local(&units, rung)` (`lib.rs:1063`).
   - `prepare_under`'s check sees the batch without its attestations, because the overlay holds schemas, units and relations only (`stage.rs:221-224`). Rule T skips a unit with no attestations (`smysl-check/src/passes/trust.rs:26-29`).
   - So the batch checks clean. Rule M keeps `derived` over `cited` or `derived` grounds, which are what a dossier's slots usually are; the dossier's own units are `cited` (`smysl-query/src/saved.rs` `to_records`, `Status::Cited`; `dossier/mod.rs:978`).
4. **After `smysl.commit`,** each such unit has one attestation, R39's `Imported` at `Model`. The trust pass reports `SMY-E033`, an error (`trust.rs:40-50`).
5. **`check` is library-wide.** It runs over the whole overlay, so every later `prepare_under` over that library reports the `E033`:
   - every later `smysl.ingest` or `smysl.answer` stages `clean` false;
   - every later `smysl.commit` fails step 6 with SM03 `staged batch no longer checks clean`;
   - SMYSL's own `stage_or_commit` refuses too (`smysl:src/main.rs:14149-14155`).

SMYSL's `ask --answer --yes` does the same with its own attestation (`smysl:src/main.rs:14209`), so this is SMYSL's defect first. But R39 A adopts the rung, and R40 A turns a later `check` error into a refusal of every Phase 2 write. Criterion 26 passes, because it stops at the first commit. Criterion 30 commits an answer last, so it would not notice either.

**Needed.** A ruling, as an amendment to R39 or a new point:
- (a) Cap answer units at the rung's ceiling before staging, as `check_local` does for ingest. This lowers `derived` to `inferred` and changes uids.
- (b) Attest answers at a rung whose ceiling admits `derived`. This departs from SMYSL's `Model`.
- (c) Have step 6, and the staged value's `clean`, judge only diagnostics about the batch's own records. This departs from `has_errors` and still leaves `smysl.check` reporting `E033`.

Also:
- ask SMYSL as U17 to cap an answer at its rung;
- add to criterion 26 that a second `smysl.ingest` and `smysl.commit` on the same library still succeed, and that `smysl.check` reports no `E033`;
- confirm all of this in M11's spike.

### B3. Phase 1's SM08 refuses a whole commit over a label the model chose, and the text ingest writes labels SM08 exists to keep out

**Location.** §P4 step 5 (lines 2434-2439); criterion 25's SM08 case (line 3375); R40 A; the Preservation rows "What `smysl.commit` writes" (line 2836) and "A label bound to several units" (line 2797).

**Evidence.**
- **Every model unit has a label.**
  - The surface and json-ast templates require one, "`kind/name` … Use a short kind such as c, d, e or q" (`smysl-ingest/src/prompt.rs:176-180`, used at `:314,353,393`).
  - The answer template says "a label of your choosing" (`:685`).
- **Each label becomes a `LabelBinding` record** (`stage.rs:97-106`).
- **SM08 refuses the whole batch.** Under Phase 1, a binding the library holds for another unit is SM08, and nothing is written (§S4, line 1186; §S8, line 1759). Step 5 applies that rule.
- **Collisions are routine, and the program cannot avoid them.**
  - Short generic labels collide across runs: `c/…` across two document ingests, and the template's `a/…` across two answers on one subject.
  - No word edits `records`, so the program cannot drop the binding and commit the units.
- **SMYSL does not refuse.** Its own commit writes the binding, and its `merge` keeps a label on the first unit (Preservation row, line 2797). Within one run, `ingest` drops a colliding label with `SMY-W054` rather than failing (`lib.rs:855-864`).
- **The text ingest writes such labels anyway.** `smysl.ingest.text` commits each window's `labels` inside SMYSL (`text/run.rs:440-441,519-529,546`), with no SM08. Across windows and runs it can bind one label to several units, the state Phase 1's §S4 refuses to create. Phase 1 words that resolve such a label are then SM08 `binds <n> units`.

**Needed.**
- A ruling for `smysl.commit`. For example, drop a staged binding the library holds for another unit, with a `Warning` naming it, as `ingest` itself does with `W054`, and keep the units. Or keep SM08 and state that a second ingest may be refused for a label.
- A Preservation row for the text ingest's bindings.
- A criterion case for a colliding model label.

## Should fix

### S1. `smysl.commit` trusts more than R40 says: `Thread` and `View` records, and the `step`, `recipe` and `family` fields

**Location.** §P4, "Nothing in it is trusted at commit" (lines 2408-2411), steps 4-8 (lines 2430-2449); R40 A; §P7 (line 2621).

**Evidence.**
- **`Thread` and `View` are not checked.** Step 4 admits them for an answer, and `prepare_under` takes units, relations, labels and schemas only (`stage.rs:179-187`). So step 6 neither checks them nor gives them back. Step 8 writes "what step 6 gives back", which as written omits the dossier's `Thread` and `View`. If they are written from the BIN instead, they are written unchecked: a hand-built value with `step: "answer"` can add any thread or view, which no Phase 1 word can write.
- **Rule M can strand them.** If step 6 moves a dossier unit's uid by rule M, a `Thread` step that names the old uid dangles (`monotone.rs`'s module header: "weakening a unit changes its identity").
- **`step` decides the rung** (`Document` or `Model`, §P7), and the staged `recipe` and `family` are written into Bund2's attestations (line 2621). Both come from the MAP. A program can relabel an answer as an ingest, which raises its rung ceiling from `inferred` to `cited`. It can also set any recipe on its attestations, and salience groups corroboration by agent and recipe (§Current, line 568).

**Needed.**
- Say how `Thread` and `View` are validated: for example, every uid a step or view names is in the batch or held, and the question unit is the batch's first unit. Or say they are re-derived.
- Say they are written after step 6's remap.
- Name the fields `smysl.commit` takes on trust, and give a Preservation row for them.

### S2. Commit re-checks under a granularity the staged value does not record

**Location.** §P4 step 6; the staged-value table (lines 2390-2404).

**Evidence.**
- Document ingest checks under the named profile only when `granularity` is named (`lib.rs:767`, passed at `:909-917`).
- The text ingest always passes one (`text/run.rs:526`).
- `None` takes the profile from the store's view (`stage.rs:169-178`).
- The staged value has no `granularity` key, and step 6 does not say what it passes.

So a batch staged at `fine` can be refused at commit under the library's profile, or it can pass at commit with granularity errors it had at staging.

**Needed.** A `granularity` key in the staged value, read by step 6, or a statement of which profile step 6 uses.

### S3. R41's guard for `smysl.ingest` rests on `clean`, which R40 says is not believed

**Location.** §P6, "When the model fails", item 2 (lines 2548-2550); §P4 (lines 2408-2411); criterion 25's last sentence (lines 3378-3379); criterion 28's `smysl.ingest` bullet.

**Evidence.**
- A failed call yields a degraded prose unit, a valid unit with a `W304` warning (`repair.rs:341-361`).
- Step 6 therefore finds no error in it.
- Only the staged `clean` flag, set false by Bund2, stops the commit. A program that sets `clean` true commits the degraded prose. Criterion 25's "`clean` set true by hand over a batch with errors" covers errors, not failed calls.

**Needed.** Say plainly that `clean` false for a failed call is advice a program can override, as its `--yes`. Or carry the failure in a form step 6 re-derives.

### S4. R41's probe does not establish what R41 needs, and it has costs the RFC does not count

**Location.** §P6 item 1 (lines 2542-2545); criteria 23, 27 and 28; the Preservation rows "Running a program" (line 2833) and "SMYSL's rule I" (line 2839).

**Evidence.**
- **A probe passes with the model missing.**
  - Ollama's probe answers `reachable: true` when the configured model "is NOT installed" (`map/ollama.rs:384-395`).
  - So do OpenAI's, Gemini's and DeepSeek's when the model is "NOT among them" (`map/openai.rs:235-248`; `map/gemini.rs:470-483`; `map/deepseek.rs:233-242`).
  - Every completion then fails. `smysl.ingest.text` journals every window as degraded prose, which is the case R41 exists for.
- **A hosted probe resolves the key** through `headers()` (`map/anthropic.rs:318-322` and likewise), so `api_key_cmd` runs once more per word. It also sends a `GET` with credentials. The "Running a program" row says once per model call.
- **The probe must come after `for_task`.** Only `for_task` applies `offline` (`registry.rs:92-112`); `probe` on a provider taken by `get` does not. Criterion 23's "counts … no probe" depends on that order, which §P6 does not state.
- **A rerun with nothing to do now needs a provider.** A rerun whose windows are all journalled now probes. With the provider down it is SM13, where SMYSL would skip all windows. Criterion 27's "run again … the script counts no call" must say whether a probe counts.

**Needed.**
- Check the probe's `models` against the model the step will ask for, or say why not.
- Count the probe in the "Running a program" row.
- State the order: `for_task`, then `probe`.
- Settle criterion 27's wording.

### S5. `ingest.prompt` is resolved against the project, not the configuration, and a prompt override can read further files

**Location.** §P3's table (line 2283) and the paragraph after it (lines 2286-2288); §P5, "What is read from the host" (lines 2488-2490); the Preservation row "Files read" (line 2831); criterion 22's last bullet (lines 3344-3346).

**Evidence.**
- **The base is the project.** `load_prompt` resolves `ingest.prompt` through `config_relative` (`smysl:src/main.rs:10715-10716`). Without `-C`, that is `project_file`, the parent of the store (`:10669-10673`, `:10593-10595`), not the directory holding `.smysl/config.hjson`. The field's own comment says "relative to the project" (`config.rs:90-91`).
- **So criterion 22's case fails as written.** With the sidecar at `/p/.smysl/config.hjson`, a file "beside the configuration" named relatively is looked for in `/p`.
- **An override reads more files.** `PromptOverride::load_file` reads each `<key>_file` the override names, `base.join(f)`, and an absolute `f` replaces the base (`smysl-ingest/src/prompt.rs:897-935`). Its contents are sent to the model. So a configuration can make a model word read, and egress, any file on the host.

**Needed.**
- Correct the resolution rule: the project for the sidecar, the file's own directory for `opts.config`.
- Fix criterion 22's case.
- Name the `*_file` reads in §P5 and in the "Files read" row.

### S6. R42's mark is not specified tightly enough to hold, or to be cleared

**Location.** §P6, "Who waits" (lines 2507-2513); R42 A; criterion 27's fourth bullet (lines 3405-3407).

**Evidence.**
- `serialise` looks up the root's `&'static Mutex<()>` under the table's lock, releases the table, and then blocks on the root mutex (`crates/bund2-stdlib/src/smysl/write.rs:55-67`). `std::sync::Mutex` has no fairness.
- **A late mark is missed.** A writer that checks for the mark before the text ingest sets it, and then blocks on the root mutex, waits for the whole run: the case R42 removes.
- **A mark that outlives the run blocks the root.** If the mark is not cleared by a drop guard, a panic in the run would leave it. The panic path is R20's, and D49 reports it. Every later write to that root in the process would then be SM07, even after `bund2 smysl-unlock` has removed the file locks.

**Needed.**
- State that the mark is set and tested under the table's lock.
- State that a writer finding the root mutex held re-tests rather than blocking, for example with `try_lock` in a loop or a condition variable.
- State that the mark is cleared by a guard on every exit, unwinding included.
- Add the text ingest's panic case to criterion 28: `Error::internal`, locks kept, mark cleared.

### S7. A text ingest's failed calls cannot be "named" by window as §P6 and criterion 27 say

**Location.** §P6 item 2, `smysl.ingest.text` (lines 2553-2557); criterion 27's last bullet (lines 3413-3415).

**Evidence.**
- **Nothing in the run names a window.**
  - The adaptor sees requests, not windows.
  - `degrade`'s `W304` names the degraded unit's uid and the error (`repair.rs:357-358`).
  - That unit then passes through `attach::attach` (`text/run.rs:445-453`), which may change it, and so its uid.
  - `TextReport` has no per-window failure field (`:96-156`).
- **`reextract` re-extracts the whole expression.** It forgets the whole alias (`:334-336`), so "a rerun with `reextract` extracts it" cannot tell the failed window apart.
- **"For one window" is ambiguous.** With `Passes::TEXT` a window makes two first calls, so "`Unreachable` for one window" means one call or two.

**Needed.**
- Say how a window is named: for example, by the adaptor counting calls within the windows in order, or by matching the request's rendered window. Or reduce the `Warning` to a count.
- Make the criterion distinguish the failed window.
- Fix the number of failing calls.

**N5.** The "How long a word blocks" row (line 2843) is per call. A word makes `chunks × passes × (repair + 1)` calls, and the text ingest holds the library for all of them.

**N6. Criterion 20 runs more than licences.** `cargo deny check` also runs advisories and bans.
- Licences: `cargo tree` over SMYSL's lock with every mapper finds one licence outside `deny.toml`'s allow list, `CDLA-Permissive-2.0`, for `webpki-roots` 0.26.11 and 1.0.9. - Bans: `multiple-versions = "warn"`, so two `webpki-roots` do not fail.
- Advisories for `ring` 0.17.14, `rustls` 0.23.42 and `ureq` 2.12.1 were not checked offline.
- Bund2's own lock decides the versions (N4 of the first review).

**N7.** `smysl-ingest` takes `smysl-core`, `smysl-graph` and `smysl-check` with their workspace defaults (`smysl-ingest/Cargo.toml:41-43`). None of the three declares a `default` feature, so Phase 2 widens nothing Phase 1 turned off.

Their appends cut any torn tail the failure left (Q51's note, `smysl-text/src/shards.rs:376-379`). So by the time R25 keeps the locks, the operator may find less than the failure left. Worth one line in §P6.
